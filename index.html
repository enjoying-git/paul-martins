01 — About

Engineering mindset built for production scale.

My career has been spent in the backend — designing the kind of systems that run quietly in the background, process millions of events, and stay up at 3am on a holiday weekend. I specialize in distributed architectures, cloud-native infrastructure, and the APIs that hold large products together.

I care about correctness, observability, and the engineering practices that make teams faster over time: clear boundaries between services, contracts that survive change, deployment pipelines that fail loudly and recover fast, and instrumentation that turns incidents into data.

I've spent a meaningful part of the last decade modernizing legacy monoliths — peeling them apart into services, replacing fragile schedulers with event-driven flows, and migrating workloads into Kubernetes on AWS and GCP. I lean on AI-assisted workflows pragmatically: as a force multiplier for reviews, scaffolding, and refactors, never as a replacement for engineering judgment.
